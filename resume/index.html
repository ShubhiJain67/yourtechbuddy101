Resume review

Upload your resume, say what you want feedback on, and get suggestions back by email. Free.

How it works

  1. Sign in, upload your resume as a PDF or Word file (up to 2 MB) and tell us the role you're aiming for.
  2. It's emailed to YourTechBuddy along with your note. Your file isn't stored on this site.
  3. You get suggestions by reply email, at the address you signed in with.
